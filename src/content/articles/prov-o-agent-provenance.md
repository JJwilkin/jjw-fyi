## Brief

PROV-O is an OWL 2 ontology for exchanging provenance as RDF. Its small core describes entities, activities, and responsible agents, then relates them with terms such as used, was generated by, was derived from, was attributed to, and acted on behalf of.

## Why it matters

Modern agent systems keep rediscovering provenance as trace schemas, citation graphs, and audit records. PROV-O shows that the durable abstraction is not a bag of metadata but a graph that can connect evidence, transformations, outputs, software agents, people, and delegation across systems.

## A design question

Could an observability schema map model outputs to entities, tool calls to activities, agents and users to responsibility, and retrieved records to usage and derivation without adopting the entire Semantic Web stack?

## Evidence and caveat

PROV-O is a stable W3C Recommendation designed for interoperable provenance across heterogeneous systems, with an incremental vocabulary and formal OWL semantics. It does not prescribe storage, indexing, access control, or query latency, and a full RDF and OWL implementation may be heavier than a product needs.
